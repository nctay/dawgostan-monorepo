# Telegram Media Archive and Converter

The project archives media from stream chats and provides a separate private Telegram bot for preparing videos shared by its permitted users.

## Language

**Archive**:
Media collected from stream chats and made available through the archive's Telegram bots and channels.

**Converter bot**:
A separate Telegram bot through which permitted users submit video links and receive videos ready to watch in Telegram.

**Conversion request**:
One user's request to prepare the video at a supplied link. The request and its result belong to that user and are separate from the archive.

**Prepared video**:
The Telegram-ready video returned to the owner of a conversion request.

**Connected channel**:
A Telegram channel a user has explicitly connected as a destination for publishing prepared videos. Each user's connection is independent.

**Publication**:
A prepared video posted to a connected channel after its owner explicitly confirms the destination and caption.
